# Pantry-bundle-planner
The AI-Assisted Pantry Bundle Planner is a project designed to help a school grab-and-go pantry organize available food into convenient snack, lunch, and dinner bundles.  The goal is to make it easier to create consistent food bundles while considering available inventory, preparation requirements, variety, and student convenience.
Problem
Traditional food pantry distribution may require students to select individual items or visit on a limited schedule. A grab-and-go model can provide students with convenient bundles that are ready to eat or require minimal preparation.However, manually planning bundles can take time and make it difficult to keep track of inventory.
I designed an AI-assisted workflow that takes a list of available pantry items and recommends possible snack, lunch, and dinner bundles.
The workflow also identifies potential missing items and helps organize the inventory into a structured format.

AI PROMPT
I am building an AI-assisted food pantry bundle planner for a
college/school grab-and-go pantry.

The pantry provides students with ready-to-eat or easy-to-prepare
food bundles.

I want to organize available foods into three categories:

1. Snack
2. Lunch
3. Dinner

The system should prioritize:
- Shelf-stable foods
- Ready-to-eat foods
- Easy-to-prepare foods
- Variety
- Filling options
- Reasonable combinations of foods

I will give you an inventory of available foods.

For each bundle, tell me:
- Bundle type
- Items included
- Quantity of each item
- Any preparation required
- Missing items that would improve the bundle

Do not invent food items that aren't in the inventory unless you
clearly label them as recommended additions.
PROVIDED inventory list 
20 granola bars
20 applesauce cups
15 tuna packets
20 cracker packages
10 peanut butter packets
10 mac and cheese cups
15 fruit cups
10 canned soups
10 oatmeal cups
20 juice boxes
